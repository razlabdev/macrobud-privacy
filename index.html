Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

MacroBud ("the app") is published by Jonathan Maxwell ("we", "us"). If you have a question about this policy, contact us at razlabdev@gmail.com.

The short version. MacroBud has no accounts and no servers of ours. Your food logs, goals, buddy progress and settings stay on your phone. We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

What the app stores on your device

  • The calorie and protein totals you enter or scan, your history, and your goals
  • Your buddy's name, level, streak and cosmetics
  • Your age group (under 16, 16 to 17, or 18 and over), not your exact age

The app does not upload this information. You can delete all of it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Photos of nutrition labels

When you scan a label, your phone's camera app takes a photo and MacroBud reads the text on your device. The photo is deleted from the app's storage right after it has been read. Photos are never uploaded or shared by the app.

Information collected by Google ML Kit

MacroBud uses Google's ML Kit to read text from photos. The text reading happens on your device. Google's ML Kit software may collect limited technical information, such as device model, operating system version, app version, performance data and per-installation identifiers, for diagnostics and analytics. Google describes this on its ML Kit data disclosure page and states that it does not transfer this data to third parties.

The app asks for internet access because ML Kit uses it for these diagnostics. MacroBud itself does not send your food logs, goals, buddy progress, age group or photos over the internet.

Backups

If device backup is turned on, Android may back up the app's data to your Google account so it can be restored on a new phone. This is controlled by Google and your device settings, not by us.

Age

MacroBud is intended for people aged 16 and over. We don't knowingly provide it to anyone younger.

Not medical advice

MacroBud is a tracking tool. It does not provide medical or nutrition advice. For advice about your diet or health, speak to a doctor or dietitian.

Changes to this policy

If we add features that change how data is handled, such as accounts or purchases, we will update this policy and the date at the top.
